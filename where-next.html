Where to go from here

In 90 minutes you found a real task, built your first agent, plugged Claude into your calendar and email, and stood up a context folder on your desktop. Here's how to keep going.

  1. Build the next skill — then the one after that

    You built one in 45 minutes. The leverage compounds. Every recurring chunk of your week is a candidate — the email you'd write the same way twice, the doc you'd format the same way twice. Open Claude Code, notice the friction, turn it into a skill. The second one takes half as long as the first.

  2. Grow your context folder

    Today you set up the bones — Me, Team, Projects, Company Context, Admin. You added a reminders.md, scheduling preferences, and one person to your Team folder. Don't try to fill the rest in all at once. The folder gets richer when you notice the agent's answer is too generic and you add what was missing. Next likely adds: your manager, your top two projects, a real Me/ profile.

  3. Make Claude part of your day

    The context only compounds if you use it. Start mornings with “plan my day.” Draft your trickier emails through it. Tell it what happened, what didn't. The more you talk to it — and the more your daily notes pile up in Admin/Daily Notes/ — the more it learns how you actually work, and the more useful it gets.
