Where to go from here

You built one skill today. Here's how to keep going.

  1. Build more skills in Claude Code

    Today you wrote one. The leverage compounds — every recurring chunk of your week is a candidate. Open Claude Code, notice the next thing you'd rather not do twice, and turn it into a skill. The second one takes half as long as the first.

  2. Do the advanced agent onboarding

    A skill is a tool. An agent is something that knows you — your calendar, your inbox, your people, your priorities — and acts across them. The advanced onboarding sets up that brain: memory, connectors, a hippocampus that wakes up knowing what you're working on.

  3. Talk to your agent

    Once it's set up, use it like a partner, not a chatbot. Think out loud. Ask it to push back. Tell it what happened today. The more you talk to it, the more it learns how you actually work — and the more useful it gets.
